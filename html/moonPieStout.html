MoonPie Stout, 2025

8.25 x 2.625in.
Stout beer can wrapper
Made with Whiteboard

From the old Chattanoogan classic, MoonPie jumped into a new market with its second installment of their Chocolate Stout. These treatments focused on expanding MoonPie’s voice into a new realm, transforming it from a household treat to a late-night experience. With their roots firmly planted in an Americana look and feel, these designs never made the light of day, but they shouldn’t sit in file folders forever!
